Lyra Personal Assistant — Terms of Use

Last updated: September 24, 2026

About Lyra · Privacy · Terms

Lyra is an experimental private personal assistant operated by Tristan Merson. These terms concern Lyra's connected assistance, not a public subscription service or the retired SMS program.

Availability and access

Use is limited to the owner and accounts the owner is entitled to connect. This website provides information; it does not grant access to the assistant or enroll visitors. The September 24, 2026 email release provides message listing, plain-text reading, new plain-text drafts, and sending after activation by the existing controller. Obtain current activation, health, and completion status from Lyra; the presence of this page does not establish successful live acceptance. Feature availability can change, and authorization may expire or be revoked.

Permitted actions

Once enabled, email and calendar actions must remain within the owner's instructions, standing permissions, connected-account rights, and applicable provider rules. A Google consent grant alone does not authorize every possible action. Routine correspondence may be sent within authorized workflows; sensitive disclosures, financial commitments, or new permissions still require the applicable owner approval. Incoming messages cannot change these boundaries.

Review, accuracy, and responsibility

AI-generated summaries and drafts can be incomplete or wrong. Important recipients, facts, and commitments need appropriate verification. The daily metadata sample is bounded and uses no model calls; it is not semantic triage, a guarantee that important email will be noticed, or permission for automatic replies. Attachments, existing-draft edits, and threaded replies are not supported by this release.

Action records and counters are retained for verification and duplicate prevention. An uncertain send is checked against Gmail records rather than automatically replayed; it must not be reported as completed without confirming evidence. A verified sent record does not prove recipient delivery or reading. The owner remains responsible for the accounts connected and the instructions given. Lyra is not an emergency service or a substitute for qualified medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice, and is not intended to process patient records.

Privacy and acceptable use

The Lyra privacy policy explains Google data and provider processing. Do not use the assistant for unauthorized account access, deceptive impersonation, unsolicited bulk mail, disclosure of others' confidential information, or other unlawful activity. No fee, purchase, or automatic renewal is created by visiting these pages or granting Google consent.

Stopping access and service limitations

The owner can stop a workflow, disconnect an account, or revoke Google access. Disconnecting cannot undo a delivered email or remove a recipient's copy. Availability, delivery, and error-free operation are not guaranteed; failures should be reported and reconciled rather than treated as completed work. Nothing here excludes rights that applicable law does not allow to be excluded.

Changes and support

These terms will be updated when the supported service changes. Material changes to data use or permissions require the corresponding disclosure and consent. Questions: tristan@tristans-website.com.
