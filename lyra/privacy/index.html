Lyra Personal Assistant — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

About Lyra · Privacy · Terms

This policy covers Lyra Personal Assistant's Google-connected features, operated by Tristan Merson for private personal use. It supplements the website privacy policy; public visitors are not enrolled in Lyra.

Current state and information accessed

The September 24, 2026 email release uses privately stored OAuth credentials and, after controller activation, supports message listing, plain-text reading, new drafts, and sending within the owner's permissions. Depending on configuration, the daily check collects bounded metadata or performs separately approved TypeSafe/Jev triage of at most 30 messages from the previous seven days. It records counters and outcomes. This bounded sample and its classifications may be incomplete or wrong; they do not provide a complete inbox review or an automatic-reply service. Current activation, authorization health, and completed actions must be obtained from Lyra's operational status, not inferred from this static page.

Information used may include message and thread identifiers, sender and recipient addresses, subjects, dates, labels, and relevant plain message text. New draft and sent-message contents, operation identifiers, and provider receipts support correspondence and duplicate prevention. A verified sent record means Gmail accepted and stored the message, not that a recipient received or read it. Attachments, remote HTML content, editing existing drafts, and threaded replies are not supported by this release. The separate Calendar integration uses authorized event details and identifiers for planning and permitted calendar actions.

Purpose and limits

Information is used to provide the owner's requested assistance: identify relevant updates, prepare summaries and replies, manage authorized drafts and correspondence, plan work, and record progress without duplicate actions. Access is not permission to use email for unrelated purposes. Lyra does not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness.

Google data is handled under the Google API Services User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements. Google data must not be used to train or improve generalized AI or machine-learning models.

AI processing, providers, and disclosure

When its permission and data-use gate allows email assistance, selected message text and task context can be processed through Lyra's existing OpenAI conversational route. On September 24, 2026, the owner confirmed that the connected mailbox is personal-only and that OpenAI's “Improve the model for everyone” setting was turned off. This records the owner's confirmation, not an independent audit of the signed-in dashboard or every provider setting. If those conditions change, email-content processing must be paused and reassessed.

The owner has separately approved sending limited personal-email excerpts and a confirmed, non-sensitive profile of relevant goals to TypeSafe's Jev model for bounded triage. This route operates only while its explicit transfer permission and mailbox and model-data-use gates remain enabled. Credentials, medical or patient information, other detected sensitive content, attachments, HTML-only messages, and incomplete message content are excluded from provider processing by this triage workflow and flagged for manual review. Screening and classification are safeguards, not guarantees that every sensitive or important message will be recognized.

TypeSafe's privacy policy states that it does not train or fine-tune models on supplied inputs. Its legal documentation describes zero data retention as a separate enterprise option. This integration uses standard provider retention; zero data retention has not been established. Inputs and related service records may therefore be retained under TypeSafe's applicable policies. A no-training commitment does not mean no retention.

When a message needs closer attention, Lyra's existing subscription-backed OpenAI worker may receive a limited excerpt and relevant task context to prepare private advice or a draft for owner review. This handoff grants no new authority to send email, change a mailbox or calendar, make purchases, or take other external actions. Notifications use existing private-delivery and quiet-hours controls. The metadata-only check does not send email content to Jev. No claim is made that AI processing stays on the owner's devices; further processing routes require disclosure and the appropriate permission before activation.

Google provides Gmail and Calendar. The owner's private server hosts the integration and its records. If private notifications are enabled, selected summaries or results may be delivered through the owner's connected Discord account. Sending an authorized email necessarily discloses its contents and addressing information to its recipients and their mail providers. Gmail content is not published to this website. No other data transfer is authorized by this notice; legally required disclosures and security investigations may be necessary.

Storage, retention, and protection

OAuth tokens are stored in access-restricted files on the private server, not in this public website or its source repository. The integration uses encrypted transport to Google and the model providers. Routine raw message bodies used for daily triage are handled transiently and are not copied into Lyra's general-purpose fact memory. Local triage audit records retain request hashes, byte counts, question identifiers, typed classifications, and usage rather than raw email excerpts.

For messages escalated to the worker, limited excerpts may be retained as private task evidence and in associated model-session transcripts. Those records are distinct from general-purpose memory. Operational identifiers, prepared advice or drafts, artifacts, and action receipts may also be retained for continuity, verification, and duplicate prevention. There is not currently an automatic deletion schedule for those private records. Retention is reviewed during owner-directed maintenance and verified deletion requests; necessary security or legal records may be retained with an explanation.

Provider logs and backups follow their actual provider settings and retention practices; this policy does not promise immediate deletion from every backup. Access controls reduce risk but do not guarantee absolute security. This assistant is not intended for patient or healthcare records.

Your choices and deletion requests

The owner can disable the integration or remove Lyra's access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete existing private task records or messages already in Gmail or recipients' inboxes.

For access, correction, or deletion of Lyra-held data, contact tristan@tristans-website.com and identify the relevant account or task without sending credentials or sensitive records. Requests are verified proportionately and handled by the operator; any retained records or limits will be explained. Support access to specific private content requires the owner's permission, except where necessary for security or legal obligations.

These public pages

GitHub Pages hosts these informational pages and receives ordinary technical request data. Shared site controls may save the dismissal of the storage notice in your browser. These pages contain no mailbox viewer, public OAuth token endpoint, advertising, or visitor-analytics integration. See the site storage policy for the shared controls.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected here and disclosed before newly proposed Google-data uses are enabled; additional consent will be obtained where required. Privacy contact: tristan@tristans-website.com.
