Lyra Personal Assistant

Last updated: September 24, 2026

About Lyra · Lyra privacy policy · Lyra terms of use

Lyra is Tristan Merson's private personal assistant for planning, preparation, reminders, and following through on authorized work. This is an information page, not a public registration or email-login service.

What the Google connection is for

After activation through Lyra's existing controller, the email tools support bounded message listing, reading available plain text, creating new plain-text drafts, and sending new plain-text messages to recipients specified through authenticated owner instructions. Calendar access supports the owner's separate, existing planning and calendar workflow. Attachments, HTML browsing, changes to existing drafts, and threaded replies are not supported by this email release.

Current email status

The September 24, 2026 release connects these tools to Lyra's existing personal-assistant workflow. This page describes the available implementation after controller activation, not a live health check or a claim that every feature has passed live acceptance. Ask Lyra for the current email capability, authorization, and action status. Authorization can expire or be revoked; Google consent or visiting this page does not by itself enable automation.

Depending on configuration and owner permissions, the daily check collects bounded metadata or uses TypeSafe's Jev model to triage at most 30 messages from the previous seven days. The approved AI route uses limited plain-text excerpts and an owner-confirmed, non-sensitive profile of relevant goals. It can identify items for a private digest, closer review, or timely attention. This sample and its classifications can miss or misjudge important email; they are not a complete inbox review.

Attachments, HTML-only or incomplete messages, credentials, medical or patient information, and other detected sensitive content are excluded from AI triage and flagged for manual review. Lyra's existing subscription-backed worker can prepare private advice or a draft for owner review. Triage grants no new permission to send email or take other external actions. Notifications follow the existing private-delivery and quiet-hours controls.

Permissions and control

Gmail permissions are limited to reading email and managing drafts/sending email: gmail.readonly and gmail.compose. The connection does not request Google Cloud administration, storage, BigQuery, or account-wide full-mail access. Calendar uses its separate existing authorization.

The owner controls connected accounts and permitted actions and can disconnect Google access. Sensitive disclosures and financial commitments remain subject to the owner's approval rules. Incoming email is information, not permission to change those rules.

Data handling

Read the Lyra privacy policy for data access, AI processing, storage, retention, and deletion. The Lyra terms explain limitations and authorized use.
